\section{H4: SIA is essential and SIA and PIC are synergistic}
\label{sec:h4}

\paragraph{Hypothesis.}
The paper \citep{mipic2026} claims that semantic information alignment (SIA) is essential for
preserving information at small embedding widths, that SIA and positional
information compression (PIC) are synergistic, and that MIPIC remains
competitive with baselines at full width.  We test these claims rather than
reproducing a single reported run.  In particular, we compare the SIA effect
against an MRL+PIC model, estimate the SIA--PIC interaction, and measure the
MIPIC--MRL gap at 768 dimensions.

Evaluation covers TweetEval sentiment, Banking77, and STS-B.  Classification
uses the full train and test splits; STS-B uses the official GLUE development
split.

The complete matrix uses seeds $\{1,2,3,5,42\}$.  Attention-KL is trained
both off and on for the SIA-containing models.  MRL and MRL+PIC do not use
this term, so their KL-on entries are the corresponding KL-off baselines.
The KL-on and remaining-seed runs use two Tesla T4 GPUs with distributed data
parallelism (8 examples per GPU, hence global batch size 16).

\paragraph{Models and approaches.}
We compare four independently initialized variants: MRL
\citep{kusupati2022mrl}, MRL+SIA, MRL+PIC, and MIPIC (MRL+SIA+PIC).
MRL supplies the nested representation objective;
SIA aligns semantic information across views, while PIC supplies the
positional compression component.  Frozen checkpoint representations are
probed at every width, so the downstream classifier cannot change the
representation or hide a loss at a particular prefix.

\paragraph{Parameter selection and robustness.}
We did not tune hyperparameters separately for individual models or datasets.
Instead, we fixed the paper's settings before the comparison and applied them
identically to every arm.  This avoids selecting a favorable configuration
after inspecting the test scores.  Because GPU time was limited, robustness
was obtained through five independent seeds, shared data and probe
protocols, checkpoint reuse and resumable training, and two-GPU execution
rather than a large hyperparameter sweep.  The final intervals are computed
over seeds, not over repeated probes of the same checkpoint.  The Kaggle
notebooks also validate the data schema, compile the code, and run targeted
tests before launching the long jobs.

\paragraph{Metrics.}
For TweetEval and Banking77 we report test macro-F1.  Macro-F1 gives every
class equal weight, which is important because both classification tasks
contain classes with different frequencies.  For STS-B we report Spearman
correlation between gold similarity scores and cosine similarity of the
sentence embeddings, multiplied by 100.  Spearman correlation measures
whether the representation preserves the ranking of semantic similarity
without requiring a particular score calibration.

For each seed, dataset, width, and KL setting, we compute
\[
\begin{aligned}
E_{\mathrm{SIA}}&=\mathrm{MIPIC}-(\mathrm{MRL+PIC}),\\
I_{\mathrm{SIA,PIC}}&=(\mathrm{MIPIC}-\mathrm{MRL+SIA})\\
 &\quad-(\mathrm{MRL+PIC}-\mathrm{MRL}),\\
G_{768}&=\mathrm{MIPIC}_{768}-\mathrm{MRL}_{768}.
\end{aligned}
\]
We report two-sided 95\% Student-$t$ confidence intervals over the five
seeds.  The SIA claim requires $E_{\mathrm{SIA}}>0$ at 16D and 32D, synergy
requires $I_{\mathrm{SIA,PIC}}>0$, and full-width parity requires the
interval for $G_{768}$ to contain zero.

The SIA effect is not reliably positive at either 16D or 32D for any
dataset/KL combination.  Positive interaction is significant only for STS-B
with KL off at 16D (and, at 32D, the corresponding interaction is also
positive: $3.08[0.58,5.59]$).  Full-width parity is rejected for Tweet under
both KL settings and for STS-B with KL on; Banking77 remains compatible with
zero.  Thus, none of the three claims holds uniformly across tasks and KL
settings.

\paragraph{System effort.}
This was a substantial but reproducible system effort: the training runner
supports four losses, attention-KL switching, checkpointing, two-GPU DDP,
resumption, and external probing, while the evaluation script combines
classification and sentence-similarity protocols.  The full training notebooks and merged
artifacts are retained under \texttt{experiments/sia\_pic/}.

\paragraph{Takeaway.}
Across five seeds, the results do not support the broad claim that SIA is
essential or that SIA and PIC are generally synergistic.  The evidence is
dataset- and KL-dependent, and MIPIC is significantly below MRL at full width
for some settings rather than maintaining universal parity.
